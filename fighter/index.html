Saved plans and sharing

Autosaved on this browser. Shared URLs contain the whole plan in the fragment, including its name, materials and both loadouts. Anyone with the URL can read it. Importing or loading asks before replacing your active plan.

Data and accuracy

766 level/stage states, 2,448 component states and 32 wingman chips from the v1.30.07 client catalog dated 2026-09-07. Screenshot checks cover Lv.95 Stage 3/5: 12,000 Combat Chips + 90 Fighter Parts per click, component bonuses and the next-stage stat gains. Other levels are table-derived, not individually battle-tested. Evolution activation costs, bag inventory optimization and server RNG are not modeled. All artwork is original game sprites, not screenshot crops. Model and asset provenance.
