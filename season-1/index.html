What happens when Season 1 opens?

Radiated Land adds a seasonal map, tougher zombies, new buildings and a Class Hall. Your first bottleneck is Virus Resistance, not squad power. Cross-Warzone outposts come later.

Before launch

Choose your class

Build the Class Hall during preparation when available. Pick Engineer for growth and team support or War Leader for combat, subject to your alliance’s policy. Class EXP starts when the season opens.

Later milestones

Expand with your alliance

Start with a Lv.1 Military Stronghold. Work toward connected cities, then deploy to other Warzones when Expedition and its Alliance Outposts unlock.

First login checklist

  1. Repair the Virus Lab, then Uranium Crystal Refinery I. Collect Immune Protein from the refinery and put it into Lab upgrades. Use Uranium Crystals to build and upgrade refineries.
  2. Upgrade the Virus Lab to Lv.30 as soon as you can. Your base does not need to be Lv.30 to do this.
  3. Check the monster’s Resistance Required. Recall and redeploy after each Lab upgrade. Then look for First Kill Reward before repeating fights.
  4. Spend Class Skill Points and claim free rewards. Use the Class Hall, Season Achievements and the season exchange when available. See which items are free.
  5. Follow alliance markers. For the first stronghold, confirm the route and monster requirements. Don’t spend stamina on an objective that doesn’t connect to your alliance’s plan.

The shipped S1 examples run 14 days of preparation and 49 days of play before settlement. Use your own Warzone’s in-game timer. Build 344 does not list Warzone 117.

Season buildings

Use these in-game construction-menu renders to recognize the new buildings. Start with the Lab and Refinery I; the other buildings serve different purposes.

Spend Uranium Crystals on refineries. Spend their Immune Protein on the Virus Lab. “Protein Refinery” in the rules is another name for the Uranium Crystal Refinery.

Repair first

Virus Lab

Spend Immune Protein to raise your troops’ Virus Resistance.

Upgrade the Virus Lab to Lv.30 as soon as you can. Your base does not need to be Lv.30 to do this.

Repair second

Uranium Crystal Refineries I–IV

Repair Refinery I after the Virus Lab reaches Lv.1. Spend Uranium Crystals on refinery upgrades and collect the Immune Protein they produce. The four regular refineries do not require the Weekly Pass; the pass adds a fifth.

Refinery I Lv.6 requires Refinery II Lv.5. Once Refinery II unlocks, repair it and raise it to Lv.5 before pushing Refinery I higher.

Requirements for the other refineries
  • Refinery II needs Refinery I at Lv.5.
  • Refinery III needs Refinery II at Lv.10.
  • Refinery IV needs Refinery III at Lv.10.

These are the building requirements. Check your milestone panel for any additional unlocks.

Resistance and infection

Check the monster before you attack

Below the required resistance, your troops deal less damage and enemies deal more. After raising your resistance, return deployed troops to base and send them out again. A march already on the map does not receive the new value.

First Kill Reward

Check the reward preview for Radiated Zombies and Radiated Elites. Prioritize unclaimed first-kill rewards over repeat kills when your troops meet the resistance requirement.

Choose the rally leader by resistance

Have your highest-resistance player lead the rally. For an eligible Elite Zombie rally, each participant uses the higher of their own resistance and the leader’s resistance. A participant’s higher number helps only that participant, not the other members.

Keep γ Pathogen stacks under control

  • Fighting above your resistance can infect troops. Monsters in Contaminated Zones can add stacks even when you meet the resistance requirement.
  • Returning troops bring their stacks to base. Too many stacks cause penalties. The base countdown warning says it can spread one-third of the base’s stacks to nearby commanders when it expires.
  • Send Recon Aircraft to an ally’s base to remove stacks or grant antibodies. The PvP help says the winner’s carried stacks transfer to the loser. Losses at the Capitol or Battery can also add stacks.

Evidence: Rally Resistance and γ Pathogen help.

Class Hall: pick, level and spend

Season buildings, monsters, city captures and events give Class EXP. Each level grants one skill point. S1 caps Class Level at 40; skill points from items are separate. Spend points on the benefits you’ll use, not every skill as soon as it unlocks.

Engineer

For development and team support. Start with Contribution Reward if you help and donate regularly. Take Stamina Recovery at Lv.5 before claiming daily free Stamina; it boosts that claim, not passive regeneration.

At Lv.15, time Build Now and Instant Research around real queues. At Lv.30, coordinate Cooperative Construction or Cooperative Research with an ally.

Browse Engineer skills →

War Leader

For fighting, rallying and recovery. At Lv.10, look at War Frenzy for planned fights and Field Emergency Care for eligible wounded troops. Save active effects for when the alliance can use them.

At Lv.30, check Rally Charge and Stealth. Stealth hides your information in Intel and Battle Reports; it isn’t a shield.

Shared Season 1 tree: Battlefield Knowledge I helps if you’ll be farming monsters early. Follow the linked prerequisites through the shared tree; hitting the class level alone doesn’t unlock a node. Don’t plan around season-only skills during the off-season.

Free talent items and where to find them

Class Reset Ticket

Claim one free at Class Lv.10 in the Class Hall (also called a Class Reset Certificate). It lets you change class and refunds all spent points. Season Achievements also list tickets at 3,800 and 5,200 Virus Resistance.

Skill Reset Ticket

Respec without changing class. Season Achievements list free copies at 1,200 and 6,200 Virus Resistance. Save a ticket for a deliberate rebuild; you don’t need to switch classes to change your point allocation.

Season I Skill Point

Use the item for +1 point, up to 10 uses. The item description says additional copies convert to 10,000 Class EXP instead. The Mutant Shop lists one per entry for 100 Mutant Shards, with limited stock and unlock conditions. Check again when the shop refreshes.

Class EXP items

The client lists 100, 1,000 and 10,000 EXP items. Check your cap before using a stack: the Class Hall says only 20% of overflow EXP is banked and mailed after the next season starts.

Other exchange sources and the paid pass

The bundled Season Shop lists extra Skill Points (50 coins), Class Reset Tickets (50) and Skill Reset Tickets (10), subject to unlocks and stock. Check the live exchange before saving or spending currency. In the shipped S1 pass group, the listed skill points and reset tickets are on paid tracks, not the free pass row. You do not need the pass for the Lv.10 gift or the listed Resistance achievement rewards.

Territory, cities and outposts

Military Strongholds are map objectives (the preview also calls them Military Outposts). An Alliance Outpost is the structure your officers place to start an expedition. They are not the same thing.

Home Warzone

Start from a Level 1 Military Stronghold. Clear the surrounding zombies where the objective requires it. Hold at least one adjacent stronghold to declare war on a city. Each Lv.1–5 city adds one to the stronghold limit in the shipped rules.

Read the declaration screen

Neutral and occupied targets have different rules. Check adjacency, current protection, the target’s Battle Period and city declaration day. The shipped help lists three daily one-hour windows and Wednesday/Saturday city declarations, with cross-Warzone city and stronghold attacks restricted to windows on those days.

Cross-Warzone outpost playbook

  1. Scout before placing. After Expedition unlocks, R4/R5 place the Alliance Outpost. Put a suitable stronghold within range and plan the next connected targets. Limit: one outpost per Warzone; up to four per alliance as slots unlock. You can’t withdraw or move a placed outpost.
  2. Garrison during construction. Send troops to accelerate the build. The first placement has a five-hour protection period. Organize garrisons and coverage before that protection expires.
  3. Secure the foothold. Once construction finishes, take a stronghold within range, then expand outward through connected territory. Use the live Battle Period and check the target rules rather than assuming home-warzone timing applies.
  4. Repair if destroyed. Ruins can’t support nearby stronghold captures. Shipped help lists a 24-hour repair cooldown and immediate restoration to 50% durability; repair doesn’t grant new protection.
Peace Period, Dark Zone and base attacks

An alliance can disable one Battle Period to protect its own objectives and get 15 continuous hours of rest in the shipped rules. It can still attack other alliances during their valid windows. The rules say Peace Period changes are made on Mondays and take effect at the next server midnight. The Expedition help separately warns of cross-Warzone base attacks in the Dark Zone on non-war days and broader attacks on Saturday. Confirm your live settings before relocating or leaving troops exposed.

Day 9 · Hero Promotion

SSR Aria → UR Aria

Raise SSR Aria to 5★ and claim a Promotion Medal. The first Virus Resistance achievement (100) lists a free medal; check Season Achievements after repairing the Lab. The medal’s description lists the event opening on S1 Day 9.

Before you promote: Aria keeps her hero level, but moves to 3★ and skill Lv.1. The SSR Wall of Honor goes away. Spent Skill Insignias and Wall of Honor shards are refunded by mail; shards spent getting SSR Aria to 5★ are not. UR evolution accepts original Aria Shards or SSR Omni Hero Shards and costs twice as many.

Avoid promoting Aria right before an important fight. Set aside shards and Skill Insignias to rebuild her stars and skills first.

Exclusive Weapons

Unlock: Once the weapon becomes available, raise its hero to 5★ and collect 50 matching weapon shards. These are weapon shards, not hero shards. Weapon upgrades go up to Lv.30, with appearance changes at Lv.10 and Lv.30.

Season activities and what to do

Season Achievements

Claim resistance milestone rewards after Lab upgrades. The track includes the free Promotion Medal, reset tickets, materials and Stamina. These claims are separate from the world milestones and pass.

Aria’s Hunting Day

Spend Stamina to start Bonecrusher Butcher rallies when the event is open. Victories can drop Aria Shards. The help caps rewarded clears at 20 per day, not the number of rallies you may start.

Radiation Legion

Check the target announcement and reinforce the marked city. The client lists attacks on Tuesdays and Fridays while active, even against protected cities. Surviving more waves improves rewards; repeated failures can cost the city when its durability reaches zero.

Mutated Zombie Invasion

A separate later milestone, not the opening Legion Counterattack. Check your live timer, target and resistance requirement before sending marches.

Warzone Clash

A later milestone ties matchmaking to Warzone Influence. Subsequent tasks include the Capitol, the Mutant Lizard and defeating enemy troops. Follow leadership’s assignments and your live event objectives.

Alliance Star

React to awards and claim from the ceremony reward panel. The shipped reward milestones are 1, 2 and 3 reactions; do not assume unlimited gifts for each reaction.

Rewards and season reset

Alliance Influence matters. City and outpost captures contribute to Influence. Final alliance tiers have Influence requirements; some also require rank or holding qualifying cities. The bundled reward group has different grants by alliance role. The Warzone ranking payout is a separate reward track.

Quick answers

Do I need the Weekly Pass to start?
No pass is shown as a prerequisite for the normal Lab or the four regular refineries. The pass lists an extra refinery and a resistance bonus.
Can I change my class after picking?
Yes. A Class Reset Certificate lets you choose again and refunds spent points. The Class Hall lists a free certificate at Lv.10. To keep your class and only rebuild the tree, use a Skill Reset Ticket instead.
Why didn’t my Lab upgrade fix a march already outside?
Bring it back to base. The new resistance is applied when it deploys again.
Can I enter an opposing Warzone as soon as S1 opens?
No day-one access is shown. Check the Expedition milestone, outpost placement rules and live declaration windows.
