⚖️ Curve 1 vs Curve 2

In client data (data/heroes.json), heroes are assigned to Level Curve 1 or Curve 2.

  • Identical EXP: Both curves require the exact same EXP amounts per level.
  • Curve 1: SR heroes, SSR heroes, and Support UR (Aria).
  • Curve 2: Combat UR heroes (Kimberly, Murphy, Marshall, Carlie, Swift, Tesla, etc.) receive higher base stat multipliers (HP, ATK, DEF) per level.

🎯 Core Leveling Strategy

Hero EXP scaling accelerates dramatically past Level 100. Level 140 to 150 alone consumes over 1.62 billion EXP.

  • DPS Focus: Maximize your primary damage dealer (e.g. Kimberly) to 150 first. One max-level DPS hero outperforms five evenly distributed heroes.
  • Tank & Support Next: Once your carry is maxed, advance your front-line tanks to survive enemy burst damage.

🏆 VS Event Optimization

Alliance Duel (VS) Day 4 is Hero Day. Every point of Hero EXP consumed awards event points (typically 2 points per 1 Hero EXP).

  • Hoard EXP: Save your consumable Hero EXP chests and items during the week and pop them on Thursday.
  • Tier Rewards: Pushing a single hero from Lv 100 to 150 on Thursday generates ~9.35 billion VS points, easily clearing all 9 reward chests.
